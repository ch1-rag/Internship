V1
Savings Pool Mobilisation
Logic & rationale
Odisha's bank deposits (₹6,44,120 Cr, FY26) are 8.8× MF AAUM. Term deposits (₹3,79,718 Cr) alone are 5.2× AAUM. Every FD that matures is a natural conversation moment — the depositor has liquidity, is making an active financial decision, and is in contact with a bank or distributor. The vector models redirecting a small percentage of the total deposit base into MF products at the point of FD maturity. Conservative (3%) requires only organic distributor engagement. Ambitious (5%) requires active MFD training on FD interception.

V2
Investor Base Expansion
Logic & rationale
At 6.05% investor penetration (28.52 lakh registered investors), 93.95% of Odisha's 4.71 crore population has never invested in a MF. The EPFO pipeline adds 1,73,160 new formal workers aged 18–35 per year. NPS adds 31,471 per year. Each is a pre-qualified MF prospect. This vector models what happens as penetration grows from 6.05% to 8%, 10%, or 12% of population — without changing the average AUM per investor (a conservative assumption: new investors enter at today's average, which will likely rise over time).

V3
Female Investor Inclusion
Logic & rationale
Female investor penetration is only 2.59% (6.07 lakh investors) vs 9.47% for males — despite women being 52.5% of Odisha's population. The critical finding: female investors hold ₹3,55,630 AUM per investor vs ₹1,82,649 for males. Women who invest, invest deeply. The gap is access and distribution reach, not appetite or depth. V3 models reaching 5%, 5.5%, or 6% female penetration through targeted programmes.

V4
Per-Capita AUM Deepening
Logic & rationale
Odisha's AAUM per capita is ₹15,581 — ranked 23rd of 35 states, at just 28% of the national average (₹55,726). This vector does not add new investors — it measures the incremental AUM that flows from existing and new investors increasing their average holdings over time. The mechanism is rising incomes, step-up SIPs, longer investment tenures, and market appreciation. The conservative scenario (+₹3,000/capita) reflects organic 5-year growth at Odisha's current AUM CAGR of ~7.6% per year. The ambitious target benchmarks against Jharkhand — the most structurally comparable peer state.

Why Jharkhand as the benchmark? Jharkhand is the only state in India with a near-identical economic profile to Odisha: mining-heavy industry, comparable per capita GSDP (~₹12,600 vs ₹12,400 for Odisha FY24), similar urbanisation, no large metro centre. Yet Jharkhand's AAUM/GSDP is 16.2% vs Odisha's 7.4% — more than double. Reaching Jharkhand's per-capita AUM is therefore not aspirational — it is a structurally achievable baseline.

Logic & rationale
Odisha has 3,620 registered ARN holders (MFD licence holders: Individual + Senior Citizen + New Cadre + Corporate). At the grand-total level (incl. employees: 7,157), the density is 15.2 per lakh — just below the national median of 15.7 per lakh (Chapter 3.8). V5 models the AUM that new MFDs would bring by serving currently-unserved geographies. New MFDs are assumed to reach the current portfolio average over 5 years.

What is overlap and why does it exist?
The five vectors each estimate incremental AUM from a different mechanism. But they do not all add to five separate pools of money. Some of the AUM they estimate comes from the same person, the same rupee, or the same action. If you simply add V1+V2+V3+V4+V5, you count some AUM multiple times. The overlap adjustment removes that double (and triple) counting.

Overlap does not mean the vectors are redundant — it means they are complementary. A new female investor acquired through V3 also: contributes to V2's investor count, may have converted from an FD (V1), and was served by a new MFD (V5). Her AUM is real — it just should not be counted four times.
Three structural sources of overlap
Source 1: V3 ⊂ V2 (definitional)Every new female investor (V3) is by definition also a new investor (V2). They are the same person. This overlap is 100% of V3's AUM — it is irreducible and never changes regardless of programme design or scenario. At conservative level: V3 contributes ₹16,189 Cr, all of which is already inside V2's ₹24,058 Cr pool.
Source 2: V5 → V2 (mechanistic)New MFDs (V5) onboard new investors. Those investors are exactly the people counted in V2's penetration target. Conservative V5 adds +1,549 MFDs × ~400 investors/MFD = ~6.2 lakh new investors — a substantial fraction already counted in V2's +9.34 lakh target.
Source 3: V1 ∩ V2 (partial)When an FD holder converts to MF (V1), a fraction of them were previously unregistered investors — they join both V1's AUM count and V2's new-investor count simultaneously. Estimated at ~30% of V1 converters at conservative level, rising to ~35% at ambitious scale.
Source 4: V4 within V2 (partial)Some of V4's per-capita AUM deepening comes from new investors (counted in V2) raising their AUM as they accumulate. At the steady-state horizon, a portion of V4 and V2 represent the same AUM dollars maturing over time. Estimated at ~20% overlap between V4 and V2 at ambitious scale.
Why 0% and 100% are both impossible
0% overlap — impossibleV3 ⊂ V2 is definitionally true. At conservative level, V3 AUM (₹16,189 Cr) as a share of total raw sum (₹1,05,240 Cr) = 15.4%. Even if every other pair were perfectly independent, overlap cannot fall below ~15%. In practice it is higher because V5→V2 and V1∩V2 also exist.
100% overlap — impossibleV4 deepens AUM among existing investors. V1 captures FD holders who have never invested in MFs. These are structurally different populations — their AUM contributions cannot be identical. Running all 5 vectors simultaneously produces a larger total AUM than running any single one.
Step-by-step derivation of the 20%–55% range
1
Compute raw sum at conservative (t=0):
V1=₹19,324 + V2=₹24,058 + V3=₹16,189 + V4=₹14,144 + V5=₹31,445 = ₹1,05,160 Cr
2
Identify irreducible overlap — V3 ⊂ V2:
V3 conservative AUM = ₹16,189 Cr. This is 100% inside V2. Minimum irreducible overlap = 16,189 ÷ 1,05,160 = 15.4%. This is the hard floor regardless of all other assumptions.
3
Add V5 → V2 overlap:
V5 conservative = +1,549 MFDs × 400 investors × ₹2,57,573 AUM = ₹15,969 Cr of AUM attributable to investors already in V2's count. V5→V2 overlap share of raw sum = 15,969 ÷ 1,05,160 = +15.2pp. Running total overlap: 15.4 + 15.2 = ~30.6%.
4
Add V1 ∩ V2 partial overlap:
~30% of V1 converters (₹19,324 Cr × 30% = ₹5,797 Cr) are new investors also counted in V2. Share of raw sum = 5,797 ÷ 1,05,160 = +5.5pp. Running total: ~36.1%.
5
Triple-count correction (add back):
V3∩V5 shared investors have been subtracted twice (once in step 2, once in step 3). Must add back: ~40% of V3 investors also come via new MFDs (V5) → ₹16,189 × 40% × (V5_overlap_rate) ≈ ₹2,590 Cr. Add back = −2.5pp from running total. Adjusted: ~33.6%.
6
Add uncertainty buffer:
Unmeasured 3-way and 4-way interactions (V1∩V2∩V5, V2∩V4 partial) are real but difficult to quantify. A +5–10pp buffer is added at conservative level. This gives the conservative overlap range of 38–44%, midpoint ~42%. The simulator uses 45% at the conservative preset (slightly above midpoint to be appropriately conservative).
7
Ambitious scenario reduces overlap:
At ambitious (t=100), better programme coordination means vectors reach more distinct populations. V5 ambitious MFDs serve more diverse geographies (less overlap with V2's urban-skewed pool). V1 ambitious intercepts more FD holders who have never previously invested (less V1∩V2 overlap). Pairwise overlap falls to ~26–32%, midpoint 25% used in simulator at ambitious preset.

Moderate preset: 35% overlap applied to blended raw sum → incremental varies by slider position

Floor (20%) and Ceiling (55%) — derivation
Floor: 20%
The hard minimum comes from V3 ⊂ V2 alone (15.4% of conservative raw sum) plus a residual V5→V2 that survives even under the most optimistic coordination scenario (~5%). No matter how well-coordinated programmes are, these structural overlaps cannot be designed away. The floor is set at 20% to reflect this irreducibility.
Ceiling: 55%
The ceiling = the conservative pairwise estimate (42–48%) plus a 7pp uncertainty buffer for unmeasured interactions. At 55%, the incremental AUM at conservative = ₹1,05,160 × (1−0.55) = ₹47,322 Cr. This is the most conservative planning scenario — appropriate for a single-year budget estimate where programme coordination has not yet been established.
Overlap function in simulator: overlap(preset) = 45% if preset = Conservative (t=0) = 35% if preset = Moderate (t=50) = 25% if preset = Ambitious (t=100) Manual adjustment: slider range 20% (floor) → 55% (ceiling) Incremental AAUM formula: raw_sum(t) = Σ [V_i(cons) + (V_i(amb) − V_i(cons)) × t/100] for i = 1 to 5 incremental = raw_sum(t) × (1 − overlap%) projected = ₹73,460 Cr + incremental
